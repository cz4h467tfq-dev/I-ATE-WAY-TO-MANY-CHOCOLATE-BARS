1 · Our city

Welcome to Kfarchima

Kfarchima (also spelled Kfarshima, كفرشيما) is our hometown. Here are its geography, history and culture.

🗺️ Geography

LocationBaabda District, Mount Lebanon Governorate, southeast of Beirut and part of Greater Beirut.
SizeAbout 5 km².
ViewIt overlooks the Mediterranean Sea and enjoys a moderate climate.
PeopleAround 70,000 inhabitants.

📜 History

Ancient times

Many civilizations lived in this area, and each left its heritage behind.

A town of thinkers

Writer Saleem Takla, born here, co-founded Egypt’s famous Al-Ahram newspaper. Philosopher Shibli Shumayyil also came from Kfarchima.

Today

A busy town where Christian and Muslim families live side by side.

🎶 Culture

MusicFamous as the hometown of great Lebanese artists: Philemon Wehbe, Majida El Roumi, Melhem Barakat, Issam Rajji and Marie Sleiman.
HeritageSeven churches from different traditions, including St. Takla and the old Deir Al Karkafi convent.
IndustryFactories make ceramics (Lecico), paper, dairy, sweets, marble and soap.

🍽️ A taste of Lebanon

Lebanese meals are shared: the table fills with many small plates called mezze.

Manakish

Flatbread with za’atar and olive oil, our classic breakfast.

Tabbouleh

Fresh parsley, tomato, bulgur and lemon.

Hummus

Creamy chickpea dip, eaten with bread.

Knefeh

A warm, sweet cheese pastry with syrup.

Find us

Where is Kfarchima?

Kfarchima sits on the hills just southeast of Beirut, between the city and the mountains of Mount Lebanon. From here you can see the Mediterranean Sea.

2 · Our school

Collège Notre Dame des Sœurs Antonines – Kfarchima

Also known as Antonines Sisters High School, Kfarchima. Our school is part of a network of schools run by the Antonine Sisters across Lebanon.

🏫

Founded by the Antonine Sisters

The school was founded by the Congregation of the Antonine Sisters, a Lebanese religious order that runs schools across the country.

🎒

From preschool to secondary

Students can study here from their very first school years until they finish secondary school.

💜

Values first

The school puts a strong emphasis on moral and spiritual development, alongside academic learning.

⚽

Beyond the classroom

Extracurricular activities and field trips, like a 2023 visit by 73 Grade 9 and Grade 12 students to learn how a national institution supports Lebanon’s economy.

📱

A connected school

Our school app keeps parents and students updated with news, schedules, homework and announcements.

🌍

Open to the world

Through eTwinning and WELNOVA, we now collaborate with schools in Ukraine, Bulgaria and Turkey.
