Our city

Where we live, how it looks on the map, and what it is known for.

Kfarchima sits on the first hills above Beirut, close enough to the capital to be part of it, high enough to see the Mediterranean from its rooftops.

Geography

Location
Baabda District, Mount Lebanon Governorate, southeast of Beirut and part of Greater Beirut.
Size
About 5 km², home to around 70,000 people.
Landscape
Hillside streets overlooking the Mediterranean, with a mild climate.
Neighbours
Hadath, Choueifat, Baabda, and Bsous with its Silk Museum.
Name
Also written Kfarshima, كفرشيما in Arabic.

History

Long before us
Many civilizations passed through these hills, and each left something behind.
Writers and thinkers
Saleem Takla, born here, co-founded Egypt’s Al-Ahram newspaper. The philosopher Shibli Shumayyil also came from Kfarchima.
Living together
Christian and Muslim families live side by side, and the town has seven churches from different traditions.
Work
Local factories make ceramics, paper, dairy, sweets, marble and soap.

A town that sings

Ask anyone in Lebanon about Kfarchima and they will name a singer. These artists all come from here.

  • Philemon WehbeComposer who wrote songs for Fairouz
  • Majida El RoumiOne of the best-known voices in the Arab world
  • Melhem BarakatSinger and stage actor who toured four continents
  • Issam RajjiSinger and composer who worked with the Rahbani brothers
  • Marie SleimanSinger, first in Mount Lebanon on a 1980 national talent show

Our school

Collège Notre Dame des Sœurs Antonines – Kfarchima, also called Antonines Sisters High School.

Our school was founded by the Congregation of the Antonine Sisters, a Lebanese order that runs schools across the country. Students can stay here from preschool until they finish secondary school.

Parental consent

Every student joins with written permission from their parents, on a form written in English.

  1. Sent home

    Parents receive the form with the project’s aims and activities.

  2. Signed

    Parents approve taking part, and the use of photos and student work on TwinSpace.

  3. Hidden

    Surnames, signatures and contact details are covered.

  4. Uploaded

    Forms go to TwinSpace with only the teacher’s name and school name.
